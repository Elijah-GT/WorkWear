        What the labels mean

        • Heat risk comes from air temperature and humidity at the helmet (the heat index), not body temperature.
        • Simulated location: the zone was set on the dashboard. Located by BLE beacons or GPS: the helmet measured it.
        • Sample data: simulated helmets and seeded history, not real readings or real incidents.
        • Pending: not reviewed yet. Only confirmed incidents count on the map.
        • Arrived late: the helmet held the fall while its Wi-Fi was down; the time is when the server got it.
